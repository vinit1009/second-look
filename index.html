Checking for the on-device model...

How your letter stays private. Second Look reads the letter with Gemini Nano, a model that runs inside Chrome on your computer. Names, member IDs, dates and addresses are masked on your device. Only if you choose to draft an appeal does the masked text go to Google's Gemini, through Firebase AI Logic; your real details are filled back in here. Until you say yes, this page loads no third-party script and sends nothing; then Google's reCAPTCHA checks that the request comes from this app (Firebase App Check). Nothing is stored.
